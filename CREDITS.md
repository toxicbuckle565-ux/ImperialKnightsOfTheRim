# Credits

**Imperial Knights of the Rim** - made by **ToxicBuckle565**.

## Art

All Knight, weapon and building pictures were painted by ToxicBuckle565, based on community 3D models:

- **Questoris** parts: "Questoris Knight Builder" by **Pedjorsi** (Thingiverse) - CC BY-NC-SA. This is why the mod's art
  is shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (see [LICENSE-ASSETS.md](LICENSE-ASSETS.md)).
- **Dominus**: based on "Castle Keeper" by **Solutionlesn**.
- Other parts come from community kits whose original authors could not all be identified (many models were
  second-hand, scanned or stitched together from several kits). If your work is used here and you would like credit or
  removal, please open an Issue and it will be fixed quickly.

No 3D model files are included in this repository or the mod.

## Sounds
