Knight stickers (hangar paint window -> Stickers).

Each PNG here is one sticker. RED, GREEN and BLUE pick the sticker's colour 1, 2 and 3 (chosen in game); alpha is the shape.
Paint your own in pure red / green / blue (shades of red = colour 1 at that strength, etc.).
A file whose name ends in _fixed (e.g. house_symbol_fixed.png) keeps the colours you painted instead.
Square pictures work best (256 or 512 px). New files show up next time the game starts.
