[h1]Imperial Knights of the Rim[/h1]
By decree of the Mechanicum, the ancient war engines of the Knightly houses have come to the Rim. Raise a forge, uncover the sacred patterns, and bind a worthy colonist to the Throne Mechanicum - then walk a fortress of adamantium and plasma into the fight.

[b]Five pilotable Knight classes, built on Vehicle Framework[/b] - each with its own weapons, armour, hangar and paint.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/toxicbuckle565-ux/ImperialKnightsOfTheRim/main/docs/workshop/header_knights.png[/img]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/toxicbuckle565-ux/ImperialKnightsOfTheRim/main/docs/workshop/knight_armiger.png[/img]
[h3]Armiger - the hunting hound of the household[/h3]
Light, fast and fierce. Armigers run down prey and screen the greater Knights, their pilots bound as bondsmen to a noble scion.
[b]Arms:[/b] Armiger autocannon, thermal spear, Reaper chain-cleaver, graviton pulsar, volkite veuglaire, lightning lock, conversion beam cannon, siege claw and rad cleanser
[b]Carapace:[/b] heavy stubber, meltagun

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/toxicbuckle565-ux/ImperialKnightsOfTheRim/main/docs/workshop/knight_questoris.png[/img]
[h3]Questoris - the backbone of every Knightly house[/h3]
The classic battle Knight, with the widest arsenal of them all - from the rapid-fire battle cannon to the Thunderstrike gauntlet.
[b]Arms:[/b] rapid-fire battle cannon, thermal cannon, Avenger gatling cannon, Reaper chainsword, Thunderstrike gauntlet, lightning cannon, volkite chierovile, Hekaton siege claw and rad cleanser, las-impulsor, plasma executor, conversion beam obliterator
[b]Top carapace:[/b] Ironstorm missile pod, Stormspear rocket pod, twin Icarus autocannon
[b]Side mount:[/b] heavy stubber, meltagun, multi-laser, phosphor blaster, phased plasma-fusil, graviton crusher

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/toxicbuckle565-ux/ImperialKnightsOfTheRim/main/docs/workshop/knight_cerastus.png[/img]
[h3]Cerastus - the swift and the deadly[/h3]
Leaner and faster than the Questoris, built to strike hard and keep moving. Field it as a Knight-Lancer, Acheron, Castigator or Atrapos.
[b]Arms:[/b] Cerastus shock lance, ion gauntlet shield, Acheron flame cannon, Reaper chainfist and twin heavy bolter, Castigator bolt cannon, Tempest warblade, Atrapos lascutter, graviton singularity cannon

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/toxicbuckle565-ux/ImperialKnightsOfTheRim/main/docs/workshop/knight_dominus.png[/img]
[h3]Dominus - a fortress that walks[/h3]
Twin plasma cores feed the heaviest guns a Knight can carry. Slow, unstoppable, and built to break armies.
[b]Arms:[/b] plasma decimator, volcano lance, conflagration cannon, Thundercoil harpoon
[b]Shoulders and top mount:[/b] shieldbreaker missile launcher, twin siegebreaker cannon
[b]Head guns:[/b] twin meltagun

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/toxicbuckle565-ux/ImperialKnightsOfTheRim/main/docs/workshop/knight_acastus.png[/img]
[h3]Acastus Porphyrion - the super-heavy[/h3]
The rarest and mightiest Knight of all - a towering super-heavy that turns the tide of whole campaigns.
[b]Arms:[/b] twin magna lascannon, twin conversion beam cannon
[b]Top launcher:[/b] Ironstorm missile launcher, Helios defence missiles
[b]Shoulder sockets:[/b] Acastus autocannon, Acastus lascannon

[list]
[*][b]The Throne Mechanicum[/b] - a colonist climbs in and pilots the Knight like any vehicle
[*][b]Mechanicum forge[/b] - research the patterns and build each Knight piece by piece
[*][b]Knight hangar[/b] - dock, refit, repair and rearm; swap every weapon mount from the hangar cogitator
[*][b]Heraldry[/b] - paint each Knight in your house colours and add stickers: shields, beasts, crosses, banners, Mechanicus glyphs and more
[*][b]Weapons from the lore[/b] - shock lances that stun and hurl back their target, conversion beams that hit harder the farther the target, graviton guns, Helios missiles that swat drop pods out of the sky
[*][b]Reactor meltdowns[/b] - a fallen Knight may go up like a small sun, or its pilot can overload the core in a Noble Sacrifice
[*][b]Sound and light[/b] - recorded weapon and stomp sounds, weapon glow and muzzle light (adjustable in the mod settings)
[/list]

[h2]FAQ[/h2]
[b]How do I get my first Knight?[/b]
Research the Knight patterns, build the forge, and assemble the Knight there piece by piece. Then put a colonist on the Throne.

[b]Do I need Odyssey?[/b]
No - everything except the plasma reactor and orbital drops works without it.

[b]The game crashed on the very first start after subscribing.[/b]
Make sure Steam has finished downloading everything (the mod is around 130 MB), then start again.

[b]Why does the game take longer to load?[/b]
The Knights use large, detailed pictures. Loading takes a little longer than with most mods.
